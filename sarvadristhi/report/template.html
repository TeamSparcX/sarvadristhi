8. Method, tools and limitations

  • Recovery walks the whole source. A native parser for the detected family runs first; a vendor-agnostic H.264 carver then sweeps every byte the parser did not claim.
  • Frames are validated structurally (Dahua: matching DHAV header and footer; MPEG-PS: complete pack and PES lengths) before they are counted.
  • Times are shown as stored by the recorder (its local clock); overlay clocks are compared with them per camera.
